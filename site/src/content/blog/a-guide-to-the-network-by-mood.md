The [index](/#index) filters by genre bucket, which is accurate but not always how you're actually
deciding what to open tonight. Here's the same fourteen entries, sorted by mood instead.

## You want something that punishes a bad decision

**[Crimson Moon](https://crimson-moon.hunt-games.com/)** — co-op runs where gear loops matter and
a bad call costs the whole squad, not just you. **[Sovereign Tower](https://sovereign-tower.hunt-games.com/)**
is the solo version of that tension: a roguelike where every branch point has a real, remembered
cost. **[Mistfall Hunter](https://mistfall.hunt-games.com/)** adds tracking to the mix — the hunt
mechanics and gear choices decide whether you're the one doing the punishing.

## You want to defend something and watch the math work

**[Iron Nest](https://ironnest.hunt-games.com/)** is straightforward tower-defense wave math done
well. **[Wardogs](https://wardogs-game.hunt-games.com/)** trades towers for weapons, maps, and
factions in a tactical shooter built around loadout decisions rather than reflexes alone.

## You want a place that exists whether or not you show up

**[Doloc Town](https://doloctown.hunt-games.com/)** runs on routines, relationships, and seasons
that keep moving on their own clock. **[Fields of Mistria](https://mistria.hunt-games.com/)** is
the farming-sim cousin — crops, seasons, and townsfolk indexed for a long-haul save file, not a
weekend.

## You want systems to optimize, not enemies to beat

**[Schedule 1](https://schedule1.hunt-games.com/)** is mix calculators and batch math — the whole
appeal is getting a recipe exactly right. **[Sandustry](https://sandustry.hunt-games.com/)** scales
that instinct up into a full factory-sand loop. **[Pax Autocratica](https://paxautocratica.hunt-games.com/)**
sits closer to a strategy compendium: database entries and run strategy for players who'd rather
plan than react. **[ZeroSpace](https://zerospace.hunt-games.com/)** blends RTS base-building with
RPG progression for readers who want both loops at once.

## You want to read a room, not a rulebook

**[Bombanana](https://bombanana.hunt-games.com/)** is social deduction — roles, modules, and
learning to read a match before it reads you. There's no mechanical mastery to grind, just people.

## You want to be unsettled

**[Grain Rot](https://grainrot.hunt-games.com/)** is the network's horror entry: harvest mechanics
nobody explains, and quests that assume you'll figure out why that's unsettling on your own.

## You want to see what's coming before everyone else does

**[Duskfade](https://duskfade.hunt-games.com/)** is the one to watch for readers who like getting
in during a demo or early access window — release notes and impressions on the systems worth
learning before the game changes shape.

---

That's all fourteen. The [index](/#index) is still the source of truth if a genre bucket changes or
a new world gets catalogued — this list is a snapshot of how they feel to play, not a replacement
for it.
